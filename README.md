# 🏋️ Personal Workout Tracker PWA

A sleek, mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for personal gym routine management, exercise tracking, history logging, and insights. 

## 💾 Storage & Data Privacy Note
This is a **personal workout tracker** that operates entirely client-side. All your routines, history logs, and attachments are saved **locally within your browser's storage (LocalStorage)**. 

* ⚠️ **Important:** Clearing your browser cache, site data, or browsing history will delete your saved logs. 
* 🔄 **Backups:** To prevent data loss, regularly use the **Export** feature in Settings to download a backup `.json` file, which you can easily **Import** back anytime.

---

## ✨ Features
* **Custom Workout Routines:** Create, edit, and reorder your workout days using drag-and-drop.
* **Prebuilt Splits:** Instantly import professional workout templates (Push/Pull/Legs).
* **Rich Exercise Attachments:** Attach web links, YouTube tutorials, or images directly to any exercise (with clipboard copy-paste support).
* **Calendar & History Logs:** Log completed workouts to a built-in calendar and history view with full date-editing capabilities.
* **Insights & Analytics:** View your workout distribution and session frequency across customizable time ranges.
* **Appearance & Customization:** Includes a dark/light mode toggle and a custom Coraline cross-stitch dumbbell app icon.

## 📱 How to Use Guide

### 1. Managing Your Routines
* **Switch Days:** Use the top horizontal pill tabs to switch between your different workout routines (e.g., Push, Pull, Legs).
* **Add a Day:** Tap the **+ Day** button at the top right to create a brand-new custom workout routine.
* **Edit or Delete a Day:** Tap the gear/edit icon next to your active day title to rename it or type `DELETE` to remove it.
* **Add/Reorder Exercises:** Tap **+ Add exercise** inside any workout card to add a new movement. Use the drag handle (`≡`) on the left of any exercise row to reorder your list.

### 2. Logging a Workout
* **Check Off Sets:** Tap the checkbox next to an exercise as you complete it during your session.
* **Save Log:** Once you've completed your exercises, tap the **Save to Calendar Log** button at the bottom of the screen to record your session history.

### 3. Viewing History & Insights
* **History & Insights Tab:** Tap the calendar icon in the bottom navigation bar to switch to the history view.
* **List vs. Calendar:** Toggle between **List Logs**, the monthly **Calendar** grid, and **Insights** analytics graphs.
* **Edit Past Logs:** Tap the edit icon on any saved log entry in your list to modify its title, date, or completed exercises.

### 4. Backing Up Your Data
1. Navigate to the **History & Insights** view.
2. Tap the **Settings** gear icon in the top right corner.
3. Under **Data Backup**, tap **⬇️ Export** to save a local backup file to your device, or **⬆️ Import** to restore a previous backup.
